# Task 6: The Importance of Patch Management

## Project Overview

This project explores the importance of patch management in cybersecurity and explains how organisations can reduce security risks by identifying, prioritising, testing, deploying, and verifying software updates.

The report examines real-world cybersecurity incidents, the consequences of unpatched systems, the patch management lifecycle, organisational best practices, and common implementation challenges.

## Objectives

- Understand patch management and its role in vulnerability management.
- Explain how software vulnerabilities are discovered and exploited.
- Examine real-world incidents involving unpatched systems.
- Identify the consequences of delayed security updates.
- Describe the five phases of the patch management lifecycle.
- Recommend practical patch management strategies for organisations.

## Topics Covered

1. Introduction to Patch Management
2. Why Security Patches Matter
3. Real-World Incidents: WannaCry and Equifax
4. Consequences of Not Patching Systems
5. The Patch Management Lifecycle
6. Seven Best Practices for Effective Patch Management
7. Challenges and Recommended Solutions
8. Conclusion and References
